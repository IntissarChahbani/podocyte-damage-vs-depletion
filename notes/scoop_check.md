# Literature Scoop Check

Date: 2026-11-03 (updated 2026-11-04)

## Research question
Does a low bulk podocyte damage score mean damaged podocytes or
fewer podocytes, and which tracks kidney function?

## Searches completed

### 1. PubMed / Publisher
Query: 10.1016/j.xgen.2026.101337
Result:
Padvitski et al. (2026)
"Cell-type-specific damage scores reveal kidney
and liver disease trajectories in single-cell
and spatial transcriptomics."

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42624116/

Finding:
Introduces the podocyte damage score (PDS). Relates PDS to podocyte
density in single-cell and spatial data. Does not decompose a
bulk-tissue score into depletion versus damage. Authors state
prognosis was not assessed. Partial overlap; not the same question.

## Scientific assessment

A damage-scoring framework and score-versus-density analysis exist in
single-cell and spatial data. No work found so far decomposes a bulk
score into abundance and damage in human biopsy cohorts. Novelty is
not yet contradicted; citation checks (items 4-6) are still pending.

Note: public bulk cohorts have no direct podocyte counts, so abundance
must be estimated (two estimators, benchmarked on pseudobulk with known
proportions).
